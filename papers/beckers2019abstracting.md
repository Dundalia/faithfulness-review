**TL;DR** Develops progressively stricter causal abstraction criteria that prevent misleading agreements caused by chosen distributions or mismatched intervention maps.
**Problem** Exact transformations can relate intuitively unrelated models through favorable probability distributions and independently chosen intervention mappings.
**Method** Define uniform transformations, state-induced intervention maps, τ-abstraction, strong abstraction and constructive abstraction; analyze counterexamples.
**Findings** Establishes a stricter hierarchy; characterizes uniform transformation through compatible context maps; captures microvariable aggregation as constructive abstraction.
**Limits** Exact relationships; application-dependent allowed interventions; proposed converse from strong to constructive abstraction remains unproved.
