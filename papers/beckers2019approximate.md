**TL;DR** Defines approximate causal abstraction by measuring discrepancies between intervention outcomes, extending exact abstraction to deterministic and probabilistic models.
**Problem** Exact causal abstraction excludes useful scientific models whose coarse predictions are only approximately correct.
**Method** Lift state-space distances to model distances; optimize context mappings; add probabilistic contexts and intervention distributions.
**Findings** Zero-error cases recover exact abstraction; establishes composition results and constructive decomposition guarantees.
**Limits** Finite signatures; application-dependent distances and intervention choices; general approximate abstractions need not decompose into approximation and exact abstraction.
