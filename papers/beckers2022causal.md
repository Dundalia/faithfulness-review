**TL;DR** Defines action-guiding causal explanations and derives actual causation from sufficient explanations, exposing distinctions hidden by independent-input assumptions.
**Problem** Predictive explanations can misguide actions when features causally affect one another.
**Method** Deterministic structural models; sufficiency networks, witnesses, explanation dominance and replacement criteria.
**Findings** Connects sufficient and counterfactual explanations to actual causes; proves independence collapses distinct causal notions; proposes fairness based on actual causes.
**Limits** Assumes perfect causal knowledge, strong recursivity and restrictive agreement with the predictor. Probabilistic, approximate and practical computation extensions remain future work.
