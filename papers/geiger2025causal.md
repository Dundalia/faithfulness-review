**TL;DR** Generalizes causal abstraction to mechanism transformations and shows how it formalizes graded faithfulness and unifies mechanistic interpretability methods.
**Problem** Interpretable algorithms need a precise connection to opaque model mechanisms; neuron-level interventions cannot isolate overlapping distributed features.
**Method** Intervention algebras; exact and approximate transformations; bijective translations; constructive abstractions; interchange interventions.
**Findings** Formalizes modular features and graded faithfulness; decomposes constructive abstraction into three operations; expresses many interpretability methods within one framework.
**Limits** Deterministic models only (probabilistic aspects set aside); graded faithfulness leaves the similarity function, intervention distribution and aggregation statistic to the use case; no experiments beyond hand-built worked examples.
