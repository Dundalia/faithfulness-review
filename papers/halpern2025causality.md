**TL;DR** Generalizes actual causation and causal explanation beyond structural causal models while recovering Halpern–Pearl definitions under suitable restrictions.
**Problem** Structural-model definitions restrict causal claims and exclude backtracking.
**Method** Abstract counterfactual semantics; parameterized witness languages; equivalence proofs for causation and explanation.
**Findings** Supports disjunctions, modalities and nested counterfactuals; recovers the modified HP cause and the HP explanation definitions for specified witness languages; permits backtracking; arbitrary disjunctive witnesses make every formula a cause.
**Limits** Witness languages change causal judgments. Equivalence requires particular language, correspondence and compatibility conditions; structural-model results concern recursive models. No empirical evaluation.
