**TL;DR** Surveys logic-based explanations that guarantee predictions or identify minimal changes, connecting their definitions to algorithms, tractability, and probabilistic relaxations.
**Problem** Heuristic explanations lack logical guarantees; apparently interpretable models can contain redundant explanation conditions.
**Method** Formal AXp/CXp definitions; SAT/SMT/MILP encodings, hitting-set duality, complexity results; probabilistic, constrained and localized variants.
**Findings** Minimal explanations admit rigorous guarantees; tractability varies by classifier; decision-tree paths can be arbitrarily redundant.
**Limits** Survey; neural-network scalability, input assumptions and surrogate fidelity remain challenges.
