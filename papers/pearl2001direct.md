**TL;DR** Defines natural direct, indirect and path-specific causal effects for nonlinear systems, and gives conditions for identifying natural effects from data.
**Problem** Variable fixing cannot isolate indirect effects or express important policies about causal pathways.
**Method** Structural counterfactuals; nested mediator interventions; path-specific equation modifications; identification proofs.
**Findings** Defines natural effects beyond linear models and derives experimental and observational identification conditions. Total effect = NDE minus the indirect effect of the reverse transition; additive only in linear models. Markovian models always satisfy the identification conditions.
**Limits** Natural effects need additional causal assumptions; randomization alone generally does not identify them. General path-specific effects can remain unidentified even in Markovian models.
