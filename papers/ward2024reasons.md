**TL;DR** Defines subjective and behavioural intention, proves their equivalence under strong optimality assumptions, and illustrates how interventions reveal agents’ instrumental goals.
**Problem** Inferring AI intentions requires distinguishing instrumental reasons from accidents and side effects.
**Method** Contextual interventions in SCIMs; subjective utility criterion and behavioural policy-oracle criterion; CoinRun reinterpretation and GPT-4 demonstrations.
**Findings** Intent implies subjective actual causality; shares instrumental-incentive graphical criteria; definitions coincide under robust optimality and specified tie-breaking.
**Limits** Single decision; shared causal representation and sufficient interventions; prompt changes can alter beliefs or goals.
