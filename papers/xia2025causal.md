**TL;DR** Introduces projected abstractions that support lossy representations while preserving well-defined causal queries, with graphical criteria for identification.
**Problem** Lossy representations merge interventions with different effects, violating abstract invariance and making ordinary abstraction queries ambiguous.
**Method** Partial SCM projections; probabilistic intervention mappings; projected cluster graphs; identification duality; neural causal-model experiments.
**Findings** Constructs query-consistent projected abstractions; derives necessary graphical dependencies; supports causal image sampling with binary representations.
**Limits** Requires a chosen intervention interpretation and graphical assumptions; added dependencies can obstruct identification; finite discrete theory; imperfect optimization lacks error guarantees.
